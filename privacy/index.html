Privacy Policy

This policy covers actitud.xyz and the personal tools I operate on my own hardware, including Whitebox, a backup service that mirrors files to Google Drive.

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and shows no ads. My web server keeps standard access logs for security, and I delete them on a routine schedule. Comments on blog posts load through Giscus, a widget backed by GitHub Discussions. If you leave a comment there, GitHub handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Whitebox asks Google for permission through OAuth and receives a per-file token (scope drive.file). The tool can only see and manage the files it created in the connected Google account. It cannot read, change, or delete anything else in the Drive. The token lives on my server and stays there. I share your data with nobody. I do not sell data, I never have, and there are no ads or trackers anywhere in this setup. Your files remain in your own Google account, under your control. Ask me to remove them and I will.

Questions about this policy, please write to esteban@actitud.xyz.
