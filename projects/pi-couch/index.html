Pi Couch

Your agents, in your pocket.

Pi Couch is a phone client for the pi coding agents running on my server. I work from the phone the same way I work from the desk: open the app over Tailscale and every session on the machine is right there, including the ones started over ssh. I read the transcript live, I steer, I answer dialogs and the commit gate, I review diffs and leave line comments, I peek at the terminal when I have to.

Nothing gets locked into the UI. Sessions stay plain pi jsonl files, so back at the desk I retake control over ssh and all my sessions are still in place.

  • Every pi session on the machine, live: transcripts stream as they happen
  • Steer sessions, answer dialogs and clear the commit gate from the phone
  • Review diffs and leave line comments on the go
  • Sessions stay plain pi jsonl files; herdr keeps them in place when you retake the desk
  • Works over Tailscale, nothing exposed to the public internet
