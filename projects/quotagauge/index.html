QuotaGauge

Every AI tool's meter, in one tray icon.

Every AI coding tool tracks your usage somewhere else: Claude Code in the terminal, Codex in the browser, Copilot in a settings page, the rest wherever they felt like putting it. QuotaGauge puts all of it in one compact panel in your tray or menu bar — session and weekly limits, reset times, and what you have left.

I picked the project up from OpenUsage after it went quiet and keep it alive as a maintained continuation for Windows, Linux and macOS. Most providers are read straight from the credentials already sitting on your machine; OpenRouter, Z.ai, Kimi and MiniMax need an API key you add in Customize.

Builds are unsigned by policy — audit the source and build it yourself if you don't run unsigned binaries. Releases are on the releases page.

  • Tracks 12 providers out of the box: Claude Code, Codex, Copilot, Cursor, Antigravity and more
  • Session and weekly limits, reset times and remaining quota in one compact panel
  • Lives in your tray or menu bar instead of a browser tab
  • Reads the credentials already on your machine; only four providers need an API key
  • Maintained cross-OS continuation of OpenUsage and OpenQuota
